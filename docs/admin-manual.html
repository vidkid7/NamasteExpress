1. Introduction & System Overview

NamasteExpress (नमस्ते एक्सप्रेस) is a full-featured bilingual (Nepali + English) news portal. The Admin Panel is the private control center where your team writes news, manages the calendar widgets (holidays, gold/silver, forex, rashifal), moderates reader comments, runs advertisements, and configures the whole site.

What you can manage from the Admin Panel

  • Content: Articles, Categories, Tags, Media files, Reels (short videos), Photo Galleries, Breaking-news tickers.
  • Nepali calendar data: Holidays, Gold & Silver prices, Foreign-exchange (Forex) rates, Daily Rashifal (horoscope).
  • Community: Comment moderation, Newsletter subscribers.
  • Monetisation: Advertisements and ad positions.
  • Administration: Users & roles, Site Settings, Analytics, and a security Audit Log.

How to reach the Admin Panel

Open your site in a browser and go to the /admin path — for example https://your-site.com/admin. You must be signed in with an Admin, Editor, or Author account. If you are not signed in you will be sent to the login page; if you are signed in with a reader account you will be sent back to the public homepage.

Language of the interface: Most labels appear in Nepali with smaller English sub-labels. You can flip every menu label to English using the EN / ने toggle in the sidebar header (see Section 4).

2. User Roles & Permissions

Every account has one of four roles. The role decides which menu items appear and what actions are allowed. The panel hides menu items you cannot use, so two people may see different menus.

RoleIntended forCan do
ADMINSite owner / managerEverything — full access to all 20 sections including Users, Settings, Ads, Newsletter, and Audit Log.
EDITORNewsroom editorAll content + calendar data + comments + analytics. Cannot manage Users, Settings, Ads, Newsletter, or view the Audit Log.
AUTHORReporter / writerDashboard, Articles, and Media only. Writes and edits articles and uploads images.
READERPublic visitorNo admin access. Can read, comment, bookmark, and subscribe on the public site only.

Self-protection rule: An Admin cannot change their own role (the dropdown becomes a read-only badge for your own account). This prevents accidentally locking yourself out of admin access.

3. Logging In & Account Access

3.1 Signing in

  1. Go to /auth/login (or try to open /admin, which redirects you there).
  2. Enter your Email and Password.
  3. Click Login. On success you land on the Dashboard.
  4. Alternatively, click Login with Google to sign in with a Google account (the account must already exist with an admin-level role to reach the panel).

3.2 Default seeded accounts

On a freshly seeded database, three demo accounts exist. Change these passwords immediately on a live site.

RoleEmailPassword
Adminadmin@namastexpress.comAdmin@12345
Editoreditor@namastexpress.comEditor@12345
Authorauthor@namastexpress.comAuthor@12345

3.3 Forgot password

On the login screen, click Forgot password? to start the email-based reset flow at /auth/forgot-password. A reset link is sent to the registered email address.

3.4 Logging out

Use the Logout / लगआउट button at the bottom of the sidebar. You are returned to the public homepage.

Security: The panel is protected on the server — every admin page re-checks your role on each request. Never share your credentials. Use the Audit Log (Admins) to review who changed what.

4. The Admin Layout

Every admin screen shares the same frame: a fixed left sidebar for navigation and a scrollable main area on the right.

4.1 The sidebar

  • Brand header: Shows the site logo (or a “न” badge) and “Admin Panel”. Click it to jump back to the Dashboard.
  • Navigation list: Menu items filtered by your role. The current page is highlighted. Each item shows an icon, a Nepali label, and a smaller English label.
  • EN / ने toggle (top-right of the header): Switches all menu labels between Nepali and English. Your choice is remembered in the browser.

4.2 Footer controls (bottom of sidebar)

ControlWhat it does
Font size — A- / A / A+Shrinks, resets, or enlarges the panel text. The setting is saved in your browser and persists between visits.
Dark Mode / Light Mode (🌙 / ☀️)Toggles the admin colour theme. Saved per browser.
Back to siteOpens the public website homepage.
LogoutSigns you out and returns to the homepage.

4.3 On mobile / small screens

The sidebar collapses. Tap the hamburger (☰) button at the top-left to open the menu; tap the ✕ or the dark overlay to close it. Selecting any menu item closes the drawer automatically.

Autopilot: All theme, language, and font preferences are personal to your browser and do not affect what visitors or other admins see.

6. Articles — Create, Edit, Publish, Delete

Menu: लेखहरू / Articles  ·  Access: ADMIN EDITOR AUTHOR

This is the heart of the news portal. Articles support both Nepali and English fields, draft/publish workflow, featured images, tags, and an AI summary.

6.1 The article list

  • + New Article button (top-right) opens the create form.
  • Search box: Finds articles by Nepali or English title (case-insensitive).
  • Status filter: All Status / Draft / Published / Archived. Click Filter to apply.
  • Table columns: Title (click to edit), Category, Status badge, Author, Views, Date.
  • Pagination: 20 articles per page with Previous / Next at the bottom.
StatusMeaning
DRAFTWork in progress. Not visible to the public.
PUBLISHEDLive and visible on the public site.
ARCHIVEDRetired/hidden but kept in the system.

6.2 Creating a new article

The form has two columns — main content on the left, publishing options on the right.

Main content (left)

Click Create Article to save. The button shows “Creating…” while it works, then returns you to the article list. Errors (e.g. missing required fields or a duplicate slug) appear in a red box at the top.

6.3 Editing an existing article

Click any article title to open the editor. It shows the same fields as the create form, plus:

  • Read-only stat cards: views, comment count, created date, and current status (colour-coded).
  • Slug “Regenerate” button to rebuild the slug from the current Nepali title.
  • Separate Excerpt (English) and Content (English) fields in addition to the Nepali ones.
  • A featured-image preview that appears once a URL is set.
  • 💾 Save Changes (परिवर्तन सेभ गर्नुहोस्) — shows “Saving…”, then a green success message.
  • If the article is Published, a 🌐 View on site link opens the live page in a new tab.
  • An Info card listing the author, article ID, and created / published / last-updated dates.

6.4 Deleting an article

In the editor, click the red 🗑 लेख मेट्नुहोस् (Delete Article) button. A confirmation dialog appears.

Deletion is permanent. Once confirmed, the article cannot be recovered. If you only want to hide it, set its status to Archived instead.

7. Categories

Menu: वर्गहरू / Categories  ·  Access: ADMIN EDITOR

Categories are the main sections of your news site (e.g. Politics, Sports, Economy). Every article belongs to exactly one category.

7.1 Create / edit form

FieldRequiredNotes

8. Tags

Menu: ट्यागहरू / Tags  ·  Access: ADMIN EDITOR

Tags are free-form keywords (e.g. “election”, “monsoon”) that you attach to articles for cross-topic discovery. An article can have many tags.

8.1 Adding a tag

  • नाम (नेपाली) / Name (Nepali): required.
  • Name (English): optional.
  • Click + थप्नुहोस् (Add). The button is disabled until you type a name and shows “थप्दै… (Adding…)” while saving. The slug is generated automatically.

8.2 Managing tags

A header shows the total tag count. The table lists Name, English name, Slug, article count, and actions.

  • सम्पादन (Edit): Edit the Nepali and English names inline, then सेभ (Save) or रद्द (Cancel).
  • मेट्नुहोस् (Delete): Asks for confirmation, then removes the tag.
Tags are assigned to articles from the article editor’s Tags checklist. Create the tag here first so it appears there.

9.1 Filtering

Use the status dropdown — All / Pending / Approved / Rejected / Spam — and click Filter. The list shows 20 per page.

9.2 The comment table

9.3 Moderating

  • Approve (green): publishes the comment. Hidden when already approved.

The Dashboard’s “Pending comments” number tells you at a glance when there is moderation work waiting.

10. Media Library

Menu: मिडिया / Media  ·  Access: ADMIN EDITOR AUTHOR

A central store for all images, videos, and PDFs used across the site.

10.1 Adding files

  • + Upload File: Choose a file from your computer (images, videos, or PDF). It uploads and appears in the grid. The button shows “Uploading…”.
  • Add by URL: Paste an external file URL and click Add URL. Invalid URLs are rejected with an “Invalid URL” message.

10.2 Browsing & searching

Files appear as a responsive grid of cards showing a thumbnail (or 🎬 for video / 📄 for documents), the original file name, and file size. Use the Search box to find by name. Pagination shows 20 per page.

10.3 Per-file actions

  • Edit: Opens an inline box to set the alt text (important for accessibility and SEO). Save with ✓, cancel with ✕.
  • Del: Deletes the file after a confirmation prompt.
When you click Upload on an article’s featured image, an ad image, a reel thumbnail, etc., the file is added to this same Media Library.

25. Common Conventions & Quick Reference

25.1 Conventions used everywhere

  • Required fields are marked with * or enforced by the browser — you cannot save without them.
  • Slugs (URL pieces) auto-generate from the title and accept both Nepali (Devanagari) and English characters; you can always edit them.
  • Image uploads everywhere use the same Media Library — click any Upload button to pick a file; the resulting URL fills in automatically.
  • Status pills: green = Active/Published, gray = Inactive/Draft, amber = Archived/Inactive, red = Live/Rejected depending on context.
  • Deletes always ask for confirmation in a browser pop-up and are usually permanent.
  • Bilingual data: Articles, categories, and tags have Nepali + English fields; calendar data stores both BS and AD dates.

25.2 Status colour legend

PillUsed for
GreenActive, Published, Approved
GrayInactive, Draft, Spam
AmberArchived, Pending, Expired
RedRejected, Live match, Delete actions

25.3 Typical workflows at a glance

  1. Publish a news story: Articles → + New Article → fill Title/Content → pick Category & Tags → set Featured Image → Status = Published → Create Article.
  2. Run a banner ad: Ads → Positions tab → create a position → Advertisements tab → create the ad pointing at that position → toggle it Active.
  3. Update daily rates: Gold-Silver / Forex → + Add → enter today’s date and figures → Save.
  4. Moderate community: Comments → filter Pending → Approve or Reject each.
  5. Onboard a writer: Users → set their role to Author (or Editor) → they sign in at /admin.

26. Troubleshooting & FAQ

ProblemWhat to check
I open /admin and get sent to the homepage.Your account role is READER. Ask an Admin to raise your role to Author/Editor/Admin in Users.
I open /admin and get sent to the login page.You are signed out. Log in again.
A menu item is missing.Your role does not include that section (see Section 2). For example, Authors only see Dashboard, Articles, and Media.
I cannot delete a category.It still contains articles. The Delete button is disabled until the article count is 0. Reassign or delete those articles first.
My new article is not visible on the site.Its status is probably Draft. Open it and set Status to Published, then Save.
A comment is not showing publicly.It is Pending or Rejected. Approve it in Comments.
Breaking-news ticker disappeared.It may have passed its Expiry time (shows “Expired”) or been Deactivated.
The “Upload” button did nothing.Check the file type (images/video/PDF) and your connection; the button shows “Uploading…” while working.
I changed my own role and it didn’t work.By design — Admins cannot change their own role. Ask another Admin.
I forgot my password.Use Forgot password? on the login screen to receive a reset email.
